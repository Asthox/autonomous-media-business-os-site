AMBOS is designed as a desktop application. Data handling can also depend on the external services that a user chooses to connect or invoke.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AMBOS processes information when you use the Application and connect supported third-party services. AMBOS aims to limit processing to information needed to provide the features you request.

2. Information We May Process

Connected account information

When you connect a supported account through an official OAuth or authorization flow, AMBOS may receive identifiers and profile information made available by the provider and authorized by you. For TikTok, this can include the information associated with the specific scopes you approve.

Authorization tokens

AMBOS may process OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to maintain an authorized connection. AMBOS does not receive or store your TikTok password through TikTok's OAuth flow.

Content and metadata

AMBOS may process videos, images, captions, titles, publication settings and related metadata that you select, create or prepare inside the Application.

Local application information

AMBOS may store local configuration, workflow state and media-library information needed for the Application to function on your device.

3. Why We Process Information

Information is processed only as needed to provide requested functionality, such as connecting an account, identifying the connected account, organizing content, preparing a publication, sending content to an authorized platform, retrieving information that a connected API makes available, and maintaining application settings.

4. Storage and External Processing

AMBOS is primarily designed to keep application configuration and working data on the user's device. When you activate an integration or feature that relies on an external service, the information necessary to perform that request may be transmitted to that service under its own terms and privacy policy.

Examples include a social platform API used to publish a video or another service explicitly enabled by the user for a requested feature.

5. Data Sharing

AMBOS does not sell personal information. Information is shared with third-party services only when required to provide a feature you request or where required by law. Third-party providers independently determine how they process information once it is transmitted to them under their applicable policies.

6. Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data can remain on the user's device until it is deleted from AMBOS, the application data is removed, or the Application is uninstalled. Connected-service access can also be revoked through the relevant provider.

For instructions and privacy requests, see the Data Deletion page.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Where applicable, you can contact us using the details below. You can also manage permissions directly with connected platforms.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data and authorization credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children

AMBOS is not intentionally directed to children who are below the minimum age required to independently use the connected services in their jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when AMBOS, its integrations or applicable requirements change. The current version and revision date will remain available on this page.
